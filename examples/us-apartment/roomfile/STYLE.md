# Style evidence

## Repeated likes

- Warm wood with visible grain
- One sculptural light rather than many decorative objects
- Graphic color used as an accent, not a full-room theme
- Low visual weight, breathable circulation, and a calm neutral base
- Furniture that looks intentional from across the room

## Dislikes and anti-references

- All-gray rooms
- Faux-luxury marble and gold packages
- Overfilled shelving
- Matching furniture sets
- Designs that hide the fixed flooring or alter the protected windows

## Productive contradiction

The resident likes both restrained natural rooms and high-contrast geometric objects. Exploration should test whether the accent is warm and sculptural, primary and graphic, or quiet and textural.
