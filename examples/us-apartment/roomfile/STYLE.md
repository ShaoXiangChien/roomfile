# Style evidence

## Repeated likes

- Warm amber pools of light and several practical sculptural lamps
- Walnut and dark wood with visible grain
- Olive green, rust, burnt orange, tobacco brown, cream, and black
- Tactile upholstery, thick textiles, and a large graphic 1970s rug
- Records, books, posters, plants, and personal objects arranged as an
  intentional, lived-in collection
- Mid-century Modern mixed with space-age, Bauhaus, and 1970s revival references
- Layered abundance that preserves circulation

## Dislikes and anti-references

- All-gray rooms
- Faux-luxury marble and gold packages
- Generic beige minimalism
- Matching furniture sets
- Arbitrary clutter that blocks circulation or radiator access
- Designs that hide the fixed flooring or alter the protected windows

## Productive contradiction

The resident likes both warm collected rooms and high-contrast geometric
objects, but also responds to quiet natural texture. Exploration should test
whether the room becomes expressive and layered, primary and graphic, or calm
and textural without treating any style label as a preset.
