# Roomfile website product register

## Audience

Roomfile is for people who want an AI to collaborate on the entire home-design
journey: understanding the existing room, discovering taste, iterating without
losing decisions, trying real furniture, sourcing products, and sequencing the
work.

The primary reader already knows that generative AI can make attractive room
images. They are frustrated by repetition, lost context, vague iterations, and
the gap between an image and a room they can actually create.

## Promise

**Design your home, together.**

Roomfile helps your AI understand the space you have, discover what you love,
iterate on the design with you, and turn the final idea into a room you can
actually create.

## Homepage story

One Mid-century Modern living room carries the reader through the entire
workflow:

1. Understand the room and the life inside it.
2. Discover taste through reactions, not style presets.
3. Iterate on one direction while preserving earlier decisions.
4. Try a real product and check its dimensions separately.
5. Connect the final room to products and next actions.
6. Continue into other rooms through editorial case stories.

## Voice

- Specific, warm, observant, and collaborative.
- Talk about the resident, the room, and the next design decision.
- Prefer concrete verbs: understand, notice, keep, try, revise, place, source.
- Let capability appear through the journey instead of explaining the system.
- Keep technical, privacy, safety, and open-source detail in docs and the
  footer.

## Avoid

- Leading with local files, schemas, commands, or “memory” as the product.
- Presenting Mid-century Modern, Bauhaus, and Japandi as the supported styles.
- Retailer-region policy explanations on marketing pages.
- Generic “AI is imperfect” sales copy.
- “Three answers to the same room,” preset catalogs, or comparison grids.
- Naming the main living-room case “Eclectic.”
- Copying user instructions into public-facing explanations.
