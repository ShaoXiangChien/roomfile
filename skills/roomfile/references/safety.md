# Safety, privacy, and scope

Roomfile supports reversible apartment decoration. It does not provide
architectural, engineering, electrical, fire-code, legal, or contractor
services.

## Rental boundary

Record lease and landlord restrictions as user-provided facts, not legal
interpretations. Treat drilling, anchors, paint, window coverings, built-ins,
flooring changes, and fixture replacement as approval-sensitive. Recommend
landlord confirmation when restrictions are unclear.

## Physical safety

Require manufacturer instructions and appropriate professional review for
wall-mounted furniture, televisions, ceiling fixtures, electrical work,
heavy objects, childproofing, tip-over restraints, load-bearing claims, and
egress. Never infer wall structure or load capacity from a photo.

## Privacy

Keep private projects under gitignored `roomfile/`. Never publish room photos,
ZIP codes, budgets, provider sessions, precise addresses, or household details.
Before sending a private image to a new external provider, identify the
provider and obtain confirmation.

## Purchasing

Never add items to a cart, buy, reserve, message a seller, or commit funds.
Shopping outputs are dated recommendations. The user must verify dimensions,
color, material, seller, price, stock, delivery, return policy, and assembly
requirements before purchasing.

## Honest output

Label generated room images visual approximations. A render must not be used as
evidence of fit, exact color, exact product identity, safe clearance, or
availability. Report remaining assumptions alongside every final plan.
